> Cockson, P.; Landis, H.; Smith, T.; Hicks, K.; Whipker, B.E.
> **Characterization of Nutrient Disorders of *Cannabis sativa*.**
> *Applied Sciences* **2019**, *9*(20), 4432.
> <https://doi.org/10.3390/app9204432>

**Changes made:** Figure 2 is a three by three grid, columns being nitrogen,
phosphorus and calcium, rows being initial, intermediate and advanced severity.
Only the calcium column was kept, split into its three severity panels, and the
original row and column labels were cropped away and replaced with our own.
Color and content of the photographs themselves are unaltered.

**Attribution requirement:** the rendered figure carries the citation and the
license in its footer, and the caption in Sanity repeats the credit. Do not ship
a crop of these without that credit.

**Worth knowing when reading them:** symptoms were induced in sand culture with
a single element withheld, on *Cannabis sativa* 'T1'. That presents far more
cleanly than a production room, where a deficiency usually arrives alongside
other stress. The figure says so, and it should keep saying so.
